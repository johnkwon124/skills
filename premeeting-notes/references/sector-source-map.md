# Per-Sector Source Mapping (from meeting-notes MODE A, content unchanged)

Pick **8-12** sources from the categories matching the sector tag. Source-category fit beats count; skip low-signal-density sources. Replace sources that carry no meaningful information. Never add low-quality sources to fill the quota.

**Per-sector source mapping table**:

```
[Common - required for every sector]
  1. Company official site (product specs, About, Press)
  2. Crunchbase (funding stage, investors, employee count)
  2-1. PitchBook (only when real access is confirmed. Quick mode: lead investors, total funding, latest funding date and Series only. Values are used but the note never carries the PitchBook source name. Harmonic AI is not used)
  3. LinkedIn (founder/CTO careers, team size)
  4. Recent press releases / funding announcements (TechCrunch, PR Newswire, etc.)

[Power Electronics / SST / Power Infrastructure]
  5. IEEE Spectrum or Power Electronics News
  6. Wood Mackenzie or BloombergNEF (market data)
  7. Google Patents (1-2 core patents)
  8. 2-3 competitor official sites (Heron Power, Delta, DG Matrix, etc.)

[AI Infra / Data Center / Cooling]
  5. SemiAnalysis or The Register
  6. Uptime Institute or Data Center Frontier
  7. 2-3 competitor official sites
  8. Major customer partnership announcements (hyperscaler blogs, etc.)

[Climate / Energy Storage / Renewable]
  5. BloombergNEF or Wood Mackenzie
  6. DOE Grant DB (ARPA-E, Loan Programs Office)
  7. IEA or RMI reports
  8. 2-3 competitor official sites

[Biotech / HealthTech / MedDevice]
  5. PubMed (1-2 core papers)
  6. FDA 510(k) or ClinicalTrials.gov (when applicable)
  7. FierceBiotech or MedTechDive
  8. 2 competitor official sites

[Manufacturing / Advanced Materials / Hardware]
  5. Google Patents (core patents)
  6. Trade journals (SME, Manufacturing Engineering, etc.)
  7. Supply-chain partner announcements
  8. 2-3 competitor official sites

[Sector unspecified or other]
  -> Combine the 4 common sources + 2-3 competitors + Google Patents (when applicable)
```
