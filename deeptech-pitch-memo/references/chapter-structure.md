# Seven-chapter structure

The memo runs Why, Where are the walls, How, Economics, Ecosystem, Risks, Roadmap. Each chapter stands alone but the whole converges on one investment view.

| Ch. | Title | Purpose | Min. length (words) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why now, why this | Structural change in the last two to three years, the core bottleneck, and the three key questions | 500 |
| 2 | Technical debate or constraint | Separate problems people think are solved from problems that are solved. State the skeptic case fairly first, with numbers, then rebut or partly accept using technical data. Give the real TRL and the scale-up path | 450 |
| 3 | Company and technology analysis | Two or three companies, each in its own section: architecture, team strengths and weaknesses, validation status (TRL, demos, customers), and whether proprietary claims can be verified. Each section follows company claim, third-party validation, interpretation. No marketing tone | 650 |
| 4 | Economics | Current cost structure against the incumbent method, the conditions under which a cost premium is justified, monetization paths of precedent companies (with business model differences), and the inflection points | 450 |
| 5 | Ecosystem and competition | Direct competitors, large technology companies and in-house threats, foreign or geopolitical competitors, and enabling layers such as supply chain and materials | 500 |
| 6 | Risks | Technical, market and regulatory, and competitive or geopolitical risk. State the conditions and path under which each risk appears, plus likelihood and impact. This chapter should be the densest and most objective | 500 |
| 7 | Roadmap and implications | Near term (1 to 2 years), medium term (3 to 5 years), five milestones an investor should watch, and a closing view | 450 |

Typical finished length: 9,000 to 13,000 words.

## Document format notes

- US Letter, one-inch margins, header with document title and "Confidential", footer with page number.
- Cover: small label, large title in a dark accent color, one-line thesis as subtitle.
- Heading 1 for chapters, Heading 2 for sections, body around 11 pt with a first-line indent.
- Use clear (not solid) shading for highlight boxes.
- When using the docx library for Node: put page breaks inside a paragraph, and use the plain package name in `require`.
- File name example: `Pitch_<TopicCamelCase>_2026.docx`.
