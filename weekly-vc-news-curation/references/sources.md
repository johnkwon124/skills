# Source list

An example list of public sources for a weekly scan. Replace the sector sections with sources for your own focus areas.

## Tier 1: General news

Scan every run for funding rounds, M&A, infrastructure investment, and policy shifts.

| Source | Focus |
|---|---|
| TechCrunch | Startup funding, product launches, venture deals |
| Bloomberg Technology | Market-moving technology and infrastructure news |
| CNBC Technology | Public market signals, large-cap earnings, deals |
| Reuters Technology | Enterprise and policy announcements |
| Axios Pro Rata | Venture and private equity deal flow, fund news |
| PRNewswire | Official press releases on funding, partnerships, M&A |

## Tier 4: Fund blogs and newsletters

Check posts from the current week only. Look for sector theses, portfolio spotlights, market commentary, and fund announcements. Examples of relevant funds: a16z, Sequoia Capital, Khosla Ventures, Lux Capital, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Eclipse Ventures, Prelude Ventures. Use each fund's public blog or articles page.

## Tier 5: Institutional research

Search for publicly available reports, not anything behind a login or paywall. Focus on the themes you cover.

| Institution | Notes |
|---|---|
| Goldman Sachs Research | Public briefings and summaries |
| McKinsey Global Institute | Full reports often free |
| BCG Henderson Institute | Strategy and technology research |
| Deloitte Insights | Technology and energy outlooks |

## Maintenance

- If a source gives low-signal content for three weeks in a row, review it.
- If a relevant fund with a public blog launches, add it to Tier 4.
- Do not add sources that need registration or a subscription.
- Verify that all sources still load at the start of each month.
