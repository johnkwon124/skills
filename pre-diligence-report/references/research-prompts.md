# Research and question templates

## Researcher, Phase 1

```
Gather source-verified information for a pre-diligence report on one company.

COMPANY: [name]
INPUT MODE: [A: from scratch | B: deepen existing materials at <path>]
BRIEF: [three key investment questions and candidate structural debates]

Mode A: research from zero. Mode B: read the provided material first and research only the gaps.

Rules
1. Prioritize recent sources. Record source name, date, and URL for every number.
2. Tags: company-stated, estimate, analysis, unavailable (with reason).
3. Do not invent data or present estimates as fact.

Coverage
- Technology: state, TRL, core architecture, verified performance and who verified it
- Product lines: keep products that differ by orders of magnitude separate
- Key market: demand statistics with sources
- Competition by camp, with capital raised per competitor
- Contracts: development funding or production order, value, term, milestones
- Team and IP: strengths and weaknesses, patents versus trade secrets
- Funding: round history, current round status, comparable valuations (estimate)
- Regulation: rules and export controls that affect the product or its customers
- Catalysts over the next 6 to 18 months
- Candidate strategic-fit points for the reader's organization

Output as tables, not prose: company overview, technology and verified performance
(with an "independently verified?" column), product lines, market statistics,
competition by camp, contracts, team and IP, regulation, catalysts.
Write "unavailable" for unknowns.
```

## Researcher, Phase 3

Answer each of the writer's five to seven questions with web-verified specifics and URLs. If nothing is public, write "no public information, reason". Append answers to the research file.

## Writer, Phase 2

Read the research file and the approved outline. Produce five to seven questions, limited to gaps that change the investment view. Skip anything already answered, vague requests, and private-by-nature items. Output a numbered list only.

## Writer, Phase 4

Write the full eleven-chapter report from the merged research file. Footnote key figures, separate estimate from analysis, give the TRL, use company claim, third-party check, our interpretation, keep chapter 10 in competing-hypothesis form, and end chapter 11 with the checklist. Save the document, convert to PDF, and report paragraph count, footnote count, and file size.

## Reviewer, Phase 5

Inputs: the final document, the research file, the footnote URL list, and the as-of date. Re-ground each claim against its origin, check tags, check for drift, and remove or tag whatever cannot be confirmed. At most two rework loops. Regenerate the PDF after any change.
